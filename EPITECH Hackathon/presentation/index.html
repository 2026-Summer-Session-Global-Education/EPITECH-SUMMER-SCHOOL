The problem

Why this, why now

📉
Newsrooms are under pressure

Fewer resources, faster cycles, and an ever-growing pile of documents to sift through

🌊
AI is arriving anyway

It is entering journalism whether or not newsrooms embrace it, often without guardrails

🚫
The people we most want to help refuse it

The journalists doing the sensitive work are the most reluctant to let AI near it

The real question is not "can AI do journalism". It is "why won't journalists let it help, and what would change that".

Where AI fits — and where it doesn't

✔ USEFUL — processing toil

Transcription Translation Summarizing big docs Searching document sets Clustering Monitoring & alerts Structuring data Draft scaffolding

A human can verify the output in seconds.

✘ UNTRUSTWORTHY — being the authority

Deciding what is true Fact verdicts Writing in the outlet's voice Editorial decisions Naming the guilty

A confident hallucination here causes real harm.

The consensus across 2026 newsroom research: AI's value is in processing information, not writing stories or deciding truth.
